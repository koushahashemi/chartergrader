Industry: Sustainable Urban Development
Company Description: Greenify Inc. is a fast-growing startup specializing in smart city technology. They develop and implement internet-of-things (IoT) sensor networks and data analytics platforms to help cities optimize resource management and improve sustainability metrics.

However, Greenify is facing challenges in integrating their existing data platform with the city's legacy waste management system.
