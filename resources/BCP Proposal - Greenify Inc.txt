BITMAN BUSINESS CONSULTING PROJECT 2025

Greenify Inc.
INDUSTRY/SEGMENT: Sustainable Urban Development
Greenify Inc. is a fast-growing startup specializing in smart city technology. They develop and
implement internet-of-things (IoT) sensor networks and data analytics platforms to help cities
optimize resource management and improve sustainability metrics.

BUSINESS CHALLENGE/OPPORTUNITY:
Greenify has recently secured a major contract with the City of Vancouver (CoV) to implement their
smart waste management solution. This involves deploying thousands of wireless sensors on
overflowing trash bins throughout the city. The data collected will be used to optimize collection
routes, reduce fuel consumption, and ultimately decrease greenhouse gas emissions. However,
Greenify is facing challenges in integrating their existing data platform with the city's legacy waste
management system.

HOW A BCIT BUSINESS CONSULTING PROJECT WILL HELP:
In preparation to fulfill their new contract, Greenify must undertake a number of tasks on which a
BITMAN team may be able to assist:
•

•

•

Data integration: The legacy system the CoV uses to keep track of trash collection is limited
and outdated. The data structure does not mesh well with Greenify’s platform and will
require mapping and a plan to transform the data to allow the two systems to integrate.
Security considerations: All contracts with the CoV require vendors to have documented
policies around data handling and storage. Greenify needs guidance on how to approach
the creation of these policies.
Software selection: One of the key deliverables for CoV is optimized route planning for their
trash collection fleet. This is outside the scope of Greenify’s current offering, so they need
help selecting a software they can integrate with in order to deliver this functionality.
