2. Understanding the Required Sections
A. Business Need for Project
Recommended length: 200 to 300 words, approximately 2 to 4 well-developed paragraphs.
The Business Need section explains why the organization needs this project and what business value it is
expected to provide. A business need can be a problem to address, an opportunity to pursue, or a requirement
the organization must satisfy. Focus on the business justification rather than detailed technical work.

For this scenario, consider the business implications of the new City of Vancouver contract, including data
integration, data governance and security requirements, and route optimization capabilities.
What good work looks like: A clear and convincing explanation that connects the identified problems to contract
fulfillment, operational efficiency, costs, client satisfaction, and sustainability. Avoid simply listing technical
challenges or prescribing solutions. Explain why the consulting project is needed.

B. Background
Recommended length: 250 to 400 words, approximately 3 to 5 well-developed paragraphs.
The Background section provides the context and relevant history needed to understand the business need and
the circumstances surrounding the project. While the Business Need explains why the project is necessary, the
Background explains the current situation and how it developed.

3. Instructions for Completing the Assignment

1. Review the resources. Read the Greenify Inc. proposal, the consolidated interview responses from Part 1,
and other relevant scenario materials. Identify information needed for the two sections.
2. Use the official template. Complete Business Need for Project and Background under Introduction. Do not
create a separate report or replace the supplied Project Charter template.
3. Preserve the template structure. You may change the fonts, colours, styling, spacing, and cover page. All
original headings, sections, tables, and appendices must remain. Do not rename, delete, or reorganize
them.
4. Write in your own words. Interpret and synthesize the client information. Do not reproduce large portions of
interview responses or turn the question-and-answer document into paragraphs.
5. Demonstrate understanding. Select relevant facts, draw logical connections, and explain their significance.
Keep statements accurate and consistent with the scenario.

6. Separate facts from assumptions. Do not invent requirements, budgets, capabilities, commitments, or
outcomes. Identify any reasonable assumptions clearly.
7. Write professionally. Use complete sentences and organized paragraphs. Write clearly, concisely, and
objectively, avoiding repetition, informal language, and unsupported claims.
8. Acknowledge sources. Reference external sources and AI assistance where applicable using a consistent
citation style. A references section may be added without deleting or replacing template content.

4. Length and Presentation Expectations
Recommended combined length: 450 to 700 words, approximately 1 to 2 pages of substantive writing depending
on formatting. These are guidelines, not strict limits. Relevant, accurate explanations matter more than meeting
a word count. Very short responses may omit important context; very long responses may include unnecessary
detail.
